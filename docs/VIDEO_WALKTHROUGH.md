# Video Walkthrough Script
## Restaurant Operations System v2.0 - Staff Guide

**Duration:** 10-15 minutes  
**Format:** Screen recording with voice narration  
**Role:** Staff member (B1 - Requester)

---

## SCENE 1: Introduction (0:00 - 1:00)

**TITLE:** Restaurant Operations System - Staff Guide  
**SUBTITLE:** Version 2.0

**Narration:**
> "Welcome to the Restaurant Operations System. This guide will walk you through the complete workflow as a staff member - from logging in to creating orders and tracking notifications."

**On Screen:**
- Show system URL login page
- Brief overview of roles (Admin, B1, B2, B3, Accountant)

---

## SCENE 2: Login (1:00 - 2:00)

**Steps:**
1. Show login form
2. Enter credentials (use demo account)
3. Click Sign In
4. Show redirect to Dashboard

**Narration:**
> "Enter your username and password to log in. After signing in, you'll be taken to the Dashboard where you can see an overview of today's activities."

---

## SCENE 3: Dashboard Overview (2:00 - 3:30)

**Show:**
- Today's orders count
- Pending actions widget
- Quick action buttons
- Recent activity feed

**Narration:**
> "The Dashboard shows you today's summary. You can see orders created, pending actions requiring your attention, and quick access to common tasks. Let's navigate to the Orders section."

---

## SCENE 4: Creating an Order (3:30 - 6:00)

**Steps:**
1. Click "Orders" in sidebar
2. Click "Create Order" button
3. Select "From Store" (B1)
4. Select "To Store" (B3 - supplier)
5. Add first item:
   - Click "Add Item"
   - Search for item name
   - Enter quantity
6. Add second item (repeat)
7. Add notes (optional)
8. Click "Create Order"
9. Show order created in Draft status

**Narration:**
> "To create a new order, click Orders in the sidebar, then Create Order. Select your store as the source and the supplier's store as the destination. Add items by searching and entering quantities. You can add notes for special instructions. When ready, click Create Order."

---

## SCENE 5: Submitting the Order (6:00 - 7:30)

**Show:**
1. View order detail page
2. Click "Submit" button
3. Confirm submission
4. Show status change to "Submitted"

**Narration:**
> "After creating an order, it starts as a Draft. You can edit or delete it in this state. Once you're ready, click Submit to send it to the supplier. The supplier will then be notified and can review your order."

---

## SCENE 6: Notifications (7:30 - 9:30)

**Switch to: Supplier's Account (B3 role)**

**Show:**
1. Log out and log in as B3 user
2. Show notification bell (top-right)
3. Click bell to show dropdown
4. Highlight new order notification
5. Click notification to open order
6. Show order details

**Narration:**
> "As a supplier, you'll receive real-time notifications when new orders arrive. The bell icon shows your unread count. Click to see recent notifications and directly navigate to the order. Let's accept this order."

---

## SCENE 7: Supplier Actions (9:30 - 11:00)

**Show:**
1. Review order items
2. Click "Accept" to accept order
3. Show status change to "Preparing"
4. Start preparing items
5. Click "Ready to Ship"

**Narration:**
> "As a supplier, you can Accept or Reject incoming orders. Once accepted, you start preparing. When ready, mark the order as Ready to Ship. The requester will be notified of each status change."

---

## SCENE 8: Notifications Page (11:00 - 12:30)

**Show:**
1. Click "Notifications" in sidebar
2. Show full notification history
3. Filter by type (click Filters)
4. Show filtering options
5. Mark notification as read
6. Mark all as read

**Narration:**
> "The full Notifications page gives you complete history. You can filter by type, read status, or severity. Click any notification to go directly to the related order."

---

## SCENE 9: Order Completion (12:30 - 14:00)

**Switch back to: Requester's Account (B1)**

**Show:**
1. View order status (Shipped)
2. Receive order confirmation
3. Mark as Received
4. Order moves to Completed
5. Invoice generated

**Narration:**
> "When the order arrives, mark it as Received. This triggers the completion process and generates the final invoice. Both stores and admins are notified of the completed order."

---

## SCENE 10: Summary & Help (14:00 - 15:00)

**Show:**
- Recap of workflow: Draft → Submitted → Preparing → Ready → Shipped → Received → Completed
- FAQ section reference
- Contact support information

**Narration:**
> "That's the complete workflow! Remember: Draft orders must be Submit to reach suppliers. Notifications keep everyone updated in real-time. For troubleshooting, check the FAQ section or contact your administrator."

---

## Recording Tips

1. **Resolution:** 1920x1080 recommended
2. **Audio:** Use a headset microphone for clarity
3. **Speed:** Normal speaking pace (~150 words/min)
4. **Breaks:** Can pause recording between scenes
5. **Cursor:** Highlight buttons before clicking
6. **Subtitles:** Add subtitles for accessibility

## Tools Recommended

- **OBS Studio** (free, cross-platform)
- **Camtasia** (paid, advanced editing)
- **ScreenPal** (cloud-based, easy sharing)
- **Loom** (quick sharing, cloud)

## Export Settings

- **Format:** MP4 (H.264)
- **Resolution:** 1080p
- **Frame Rate:** 30 fps
- **Audio:** AAC, 192kbps
- **Quality:** High (8-12 Mbps for 1080p)
