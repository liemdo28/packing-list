Chapter 1
System Overview

The Packing List system manages inter-store transfer orders between the restaurant's three locations. It enforces a structured workflow so every transfer is tracked, priced, and reconciled — from the moment a request is drafted until it is financially closed.

📦
Transfer Orders

Request goods from another store. The system tracks who created the request, who prepared the shipment, and who confirmed receipt.

🔄
6-Step Workflow

Every order moves through Draft → Submitted → Preparing → Shipping → Received → Completed. Each step is locked to the correct store and role.

💰
Price Snapshot

When an order is completed, current prices are frozen into the order record. This ensures monthly summaries always reflect the price at time of transfer.

🔔
Real-time Notifications

Each status change triggers notifications to the relevant store staff so no order is accidentally forgotten.

ℹ️
One action at a time

The interface only shows the buttons your role is allowed to press for the current status. If you see no action buttons, the order is waiting for a different store or role to continue.

Key Terms

TermMeaning
Order / Transfer OrderA request to move goods from one store (From) to another (To).
From StoreThe source — the store that packs and sends the goods.
To StoreThe destination — the store that receives and confirms the goods.
Order LinesIndividual item rows inside an order (item, quantity, notes).
Recipient NameOptional: the person at the destination who will physically receive the delivery.
Price SnapshotThe frozen price captured from the Price Master at the moment an order is completed.
Audit LogAn immutable record of every action taken on the system, visible to admins.
Chapter 2
Stores & Transfer Rules

The system operates across three store locations. Not all transfers are permitted — the table below shows every allowed route.

Store Locations

CodeStore RoleDescription
B1 Main / Central Kitchen Primary food preparation hub. Sends most items to B2 and B3. Also receives noodles from B3.
B2 Outlet Store Receives goods from B1 (general items) and B3 (noodles). Does not initiate transfers to other stores.
B3 Noodle Supplier / Warehouse Specialises in noodle production. Sends noodles to B1 and B2. Receives general supplies from B1.

Allowed Transfer Routes

B1→B2

B1 sends general items (Beverages, Food, Supplies) to the B2 outlet.

B1→B3

B1 sends general supplies and ingredients to B3 warehouse.

B3→B1

B3 sends Noodles category items back to B1 central kitchen.

B3→B2

B3 sends Noodles category items directly to the B2 outlet.

⚠️
Item category restriction

When the From Store is B3, only items in the Noodles category can be added to the order. When the From Store is B1, all non-Noodle categories are available. The system enforces this automatically in the item selection dropdown.

🚫
B2 cannot initiate transfers

B2 is a receiving-only store. B2 staff cannot create new orders — they only confirm receipt and complete incoming shipments from B1 or B3.

Chapter 3
User Roles & Permissions

Every user is assigned one role. The role determines which screens are visible, which orders appear in their queue, and which action buttons are shown.

Admin

Full access across all stores

Can see and act on all orders for all stores. Has access to user management, audit logs, pricing, and reporting tools.

  • Create orders between any allowed store pair
  • Perform all workflow actions (submit, prepare, ship, receive, complete, cancel)
  • Manage users, stores, items, and prices
  • View audit logs and monthly summary reports
  • Manage invoices and reconcile them
B1 Staff

B1 store operations

  • Create orders where B1 or B3 is the source
  • Submit, prepare, and ship orders from B1
  • Confirm receipt of orders arriving at B1
  • Complete B1-destination orders
  • Cancel draft/submitted/preparing orders from B1
B2 Staff

B2 receiving only

  • View all orders arriving at B2
  • Confirm receipt of shipped orders at B2
  • Complete B2-destination orders
  • Cannot create, submit, prepare, or ship
B3 Staff

B3 store operations

  • Create orders where B1 or B3 is the source
  • Submit, prepare, and ship orders from B3
  • Confirm receipt of orders arriving at B3
  • Complete B3-destination orders
  • Cancel draft/submitted/preparing orders from B3
Accountant

Finance review — read + complete

Focused on financial reconciliation. Can view all orders and complete received orders, but cannot create or move orders through operational steps.

  • View all orders across all stores (read-only for operational steps)
  • Complete orders that have been received (finalises the price snapshot)
  • Create orders (admin-level store selection)
  • View and reconcile invoices
  • View monthly summary reports

Chapter 5
The Order Workflow

Every transfer order follows a strict, linear 6-step lifecycle. Each step is owned by a specific store and role — you will only see the action button when the order is in your step.

1
Draft
Sender
2
Submitted
Sender
3

Sender
4
Shipping
In transit
5
Received
Receiver
6
Completed
Receiver / Admin
1
Draft — Order created, not yet sent

The order exists in the system but has not been officially submitted to the sender's workflow. It can still be fully edited — items, quantities, notes, and recipient name can all be changed. The sender store staff created this order.

Who acts next: Sender (B1 or B3 staff, or Admin) → click Submit Order
2
Submitted — Locked and queued for preparation

The order is confirmed and locked for editing. The sender store now knows they need to prepare this shipment. It appears in the sender's "Needs My Action" queue.

Who acts next: Sender (B1 or B3 staff, or Admin) → click Start Preparing
3
Preparing — Sender is packing the items

The sender store is physically packing and checking the goods. Staff should verify quantities against the order lines before proceeding. When packing is complete, clicking Complete Preparing automatically advances the order to Shipping status — signalling to the receiver that the goods are on the way.

Who acts next: Sender (B1 or B3 staff, or Admin) → click Complete Preparing
4
Shipping — Goods in transit to destination

The sender has finished packing. The receiver's dashboard now shows an "📦 Incoming Shipment" banner with the sender's name and the recipient name (if set). Staff at the destination store should prepare to receive the goods. No editing is possible at this stage.

Who acts next: Receiver (B1 / B2 / B3 staff at destination, or Admin) → click Confirm Receipt
5
Received — Destination store confirmed receipt

The receiving store has physically verified the goods. The order is now pending final financial completion. Prices are not yet frozen.

Who acts next: Receiver or Accountant → click Complete Order
6
Completed — Financially closed

When an order is completed, the system automatically snapshots the current price from the Price Master for every item in the order. The unit price, line total, and order total are frozen permanently. Completed orders are included in monthly summary calculations. This action cannot be undone.

Terminal state — no further actions available.
❌
Cancellation

Orders can be cancelled at any point during Draft, Submitted, or Preparing stages. Once an order reaches Shipping it cannot be cancelled — only the full receive → complete path remains. When cancelling, you may optionally enter a reason which is recorded in the order.

Chapter 6
Creating a New Order

B1 staff, B3 staff, and Admins can create new transfer orders. Navigate to Orders → New Transfer Order in the sidebar.

Step-by-Step

1
Select From Store (Source)

Choose the store that will pack and send the goods. Non-admin users see only their allowed sources: B1 staff can only select B3 as source (since B1 receives noodles from B3); B3 staff can only select B1. Admins and accountants can choose any valid source.

2
Select To Store (Destination)

For B1, B2, and B3 staff, the destination is automatically locked to their own store. Admins can freely choose the destination from the allowed routes for the selected source.

3
Enter Recipient Name (optional)

Optionally enter the name of the person at the destination who will receive the physical delivery. This name is displayed as an "Incoming Shipment" banner at the destination store when the order is in Shipping status. Maximum 100 characters.

4
Add Notes (optional)

Free-text notes visible to all users who view the order. Use this for special handling instructions, urgency indicators, or context.

5
Add Items

Click Add Item to add rows. For each row:
• Item — Select from the dropdown. Items are filtered by category based on the From Store.
• Quantity — Enter the quantity (supports decimal values, e.g. 1.5).
• Notes — Optional per-line note (e.g. "urgent", "half portion").
At least one item with a quantity greater than zero is required. Click the trash icon to remove a line (minimum 1 line required).

6
Click Create Order

The order is saved as a Draft and you are redirected to the order detail page. From there you can review it and submit.

✏️
Editing a draft

While the order is in Draft status, the Edit Draft button is available on the order detail page. Editing replaces all order lines — re-add every item you want to keep. Once submitted, the order is locked and cannot be edited.

Order Number Format

Every order receives an automatically generated order number in the format:

PL-YYYYMMDD-NNN

For example, PL-20260507-003 means the 3rd order created on 7 May 2026. The sequence resets daily.

Chapter 7
Order Detail & Actions

The Order Detail page is the primary workspace for moving an order through its lifecycle. It shows the current status, the timeline, all items, and the action panel.

Page Layout

SectionContents
HeaderOrder number, From → To stores, current status badge, progress timeline, cancel reason (if cancelled).
Workflow PanelShows the next recommended action and a helper description. If you cannot act on this order, it tells you why (e.g. "Only the destination store can confirm receipt").
Incoming Shipment BannerAppears when status is Shipping. Shows the sender's name and the recipient name at the destination.
Order Lines TableAll items with quantities. Unit Price and Total columns are filled in once the order is completed (price snapshot).

Actions PanelContains all buttons you are allowed to use at the current status. Other roles' buttons are hidden.

Action Buttons

Submit Order

Locks the draft and queues it for preparation. The order can no longer be edited.

Available when: Draft · Roles: Admin, B1 (from B1), B3 (from B3)
Start Preparing

Signals the sender has started packing. The order moves to Preparing status.

Available when: Submitted · Roles: Admin, B1 (from B1), B3 (from B3)
Complete Preparing

Marks packing as done. The order automatically moves to Shipping, notifying the destination store that goods are on the way.

Available when: Preparing · Roles: Admin, B1 (from B1), B3 (from B3)
Confirm Receipt

The destination store confirms the goods have arrived. The order moves to Received status.

Available when: Shipping · Roles: Admin, B1/B2/B3 (at destination store only)
Complete Order

High-impact action. Freezes the current prices into the order (price snapshot), calculates all line totals and the grand total, and closes the order. Included in monthly summaries. Cannot be undone.

Available when: Received · Roles: Admin, B1/B2/B3 (at destination store), Accountant
Edit Draft

Opens the edit form to update order lines. Only available while the order is still a draft.

Available when: Draft · Roles: Admin, B1 (from B1), B3 (from B3)
Cancel Order

Permanently stops this transfer. A confirmation dialog asks for an optional cancel reason, which is recorded on the order. Cannot be undone. Cancelled orders are not included in summaries.

Available when: Draft / Submitted / Preparing · Roles: Admin, B1 (from B1), B3 (from B3)

Chapters 9–12
Role-Specific Workflows

Chapter 9 — B1 Staff Daily Workflow

🏪
B1 responsibilities

B1 is the main hub. It sends goods to B2 and B3, and receives noodles from B3. B1 staff handle both the sender side (submit → prepare → ship) and the receiver side (receive → complete) for noodle orders from B3.

Sending goods (B1 → B2 or B1 → B3)

  1. Go to Orders → New Transfer Order.
  2. Set From Store = B1, To Store = B2 or B3.
  3. Add items (non-noodle categories), set quantities. Optionally enter recipient name and notes.
  4. Click Create Order → you are redirected to the draft.
  5. Review the draft, then click Submit Order.
  6. When ready to pack, click Start Preparing.
  7. Pack the goods, then click Complete Preparing. The order changes to Shipping — the destination store is notified.
  8. Wait for the destination store to confirm receipt and complete the order.

Receiving noodles (B3 → B1)

  1. When the "📦 Incoming Shipment" banner appears for a B3→B1 order, prepare to receive.
  2. Open the order. Verify items match what was physically received.
  3. Click Confirm Receipt.
  4. Click Complete Order to freeze prices and close the order.

Chapter 10 — B3 Staff Daily Workflow

🏭
B3 responsibilities

B3 is the noodle warehouse. It sends noodles to B1 and B2, and receives general supplies from B1. B3 workflow mirrors B1 but for noodle-category items.

Sending noodles (B3 → B1 or B3 → B2)

  1. Go to Orders → New Transfer Order.
  2. Set From Store = B3. Only Noodles category items are available.
  3. Add noodle items and quantities. Enter recipient name if needed.
  4. Click Create Order, then Submit Order.
  5. When ready: Start Preparing → pack noodles → Complete Preparing.
  6. The destination store receives an incoming shipment notification.

Receiving general supplies (B1 → B3)

  1. Open incoming B1→B3 orders in the Waiting on Other Store tab when they reach Shipping.
  2. Click Confirm Receipt, then Complete Order.

Chapter 11 — B2 Staff Daily Workflow

🛍️
B2 responsibilities

B2 is a receiving-only outlet. B2 staff only confirm receipt and complete orders. They cannot create or prepare orders.

  1. Check the Dashboard or Incoming Shipments attention card for orders arriving at B2.
  2. Open an order showing the "📦 Incoming Shipment" banner.
  3. Verify all items are physically present. Note any discrepancies in the notes and contact your Admin.
  4. Click Confirm Receipt.
  5. Click Complete Order to finalise and freeze prices.

Chapter 12 — Accountant Workflow

📊
Accountant focus

Accountants primarily use the system for financial review. Their key action is completing received orders to lock in prices, and reviewing the monthly summary.

  1. Check the Need Confirmation attention card on the Dashboard for orders at Received status.
  2. Open each received order. Review line items and verify they align with any physical delivery notes.
  3. Click Complete Order to freeze the price snapshot and include it in the monthly summary.
  4. Navigate to Summary to review monthly transfer totals by store pair.
  5. Navigate to Invoices to review and reconcile invoices.

Chapter 15
Items & Price Management

Items are the goods that appear in order lines. Prices are managed separately in the Price Master and are versioned by date. Both are admin-managed.

Items

Navigate to Items in the sidebar (admin only).

FieldDescription
CodeUnique short identifier (e.g. NM-001)
NameFull display name visible in dropdowns
CategoryBeverages, Food, Supplies, or Noodles — controls which store can include the item
UnitUnit of measurement (e.g. kg, pack, bottle)
ActiveInactive items do not appear in order creation dropdowns

Price Master

Navigate to Prices in the sidebar (admin only). Each price record has:

FieldDescription
ItemWhich item this price applies to
PriceUnit price in VND
Effective DateThe date from which this price is active
End DateOptional — the last date this price is valid. Null means open-ended.
ActiveOnly active price records are used for snapshots
📌
How price snapshots work

When you click Complete Order, the system finds the most recent active price record for each item where effective_date ≤ today and either end_date is null or ≥ today. This price is written into the order line and the order total is calculated. The Price Master can then change without affecting historical order records.

Pricing Dashboard

Admins can access Admin → Pricing Dashboard to:

  • View a summary of current active prices for all items
  • Identify items without an active price (these will snapshot as ₫0)
  • Review price history per item
Chapter 16
Invoices

The Invoices module tracks external purchase invoices paid on behalf of stores. Accessible to Admins and Accountants.

Invoice Statuses

StatusMeaning
PendingInvoice recorded but not yet reviewed against payments.
ReconciledInvoice matched and confirmed against actual payments.
DisputedA discrepancy was found — requires follow-up.

Creating an Invoice

  1. Navigate to Invoices → New Invoice.
  2. Select the store that paid the invoice (Paid By Store) and the store it was paid on behalf of (On Behalf Of Store).
  3. Enter invoice date, reference number, and add line items with amounts.
  4. Save the invoice as Pending.

Reconciling an Invoice

  1. Open the invoice and click Reconcile.
  2. Review line items against the actual purchase receipt.
  3. Confirm to mark as Reconciled, or flag as Disputed if amounts differ.
Chapter 17
Monthly Summary

The Summary page provides a monthly financial overview of all completed transfer orders. Accessible to Admins and Accountants.

⚠️
Only completed orders count

The monthly summary only includes orders with Completed status. Orders that are received but not yet completed, or cancelled, do not appear in the totals. Make sure to complete all received orders before running your monthly reconciliation.

Summary Breakdown

The summary shows totals grouped by:

  • Month — select the month and year using the date filter
  • Store pair — e.g. B1→B2, B3→B1, etc.
  • Item — total quantity and total value transferred per item

All values are based on the price snapshot captured at the time each order was completed.

Chapter 18
Admin Tools

The Admin section is only visible to users with the Admin role.

User Management

Navigate to Admin → Users.

  • Create new user accounts with a username, password, role, and associated store.
  • Edit existing users — change role, update password, or deactivate the account.
  • Deactivated users cannot log in.
🔑
Password policy

When creating or editing a user, the password is automatically hashed before storage. Admins never see plain-text passwords. Set a strong password (minimum 8 characters) and share it securely with the user.

Store Management

Navigate to Admin → Stores to view and edit store details (code, name, address, phone). Store codes (B1, B2, B3) are fixed — changing codes would break transfer rules.

Audit Log

Navigate to Admin → Audit Logs to see a complete, immutable record of every action performed in the system. Each entry records:

  • Timestamp
  • User who performed the action
  • Action type (create, submit, prepare, ship, receive, complete, cancel, etc.)
  • Target resource (order ID, user ID, etc.)
  • IP address and user agent

Audit logs cannot be deleted or modified. Use them to investigate disputes or track who took a specific action.

Pricing Dashboard

Navigate to Admin → Pricing Dashboard for a visual overview of the Price Master — active prices, price gaps (items with no current price), and price history graphs per item.

✅
Before month-end checklist

1. Complete all orders at Received status.
2. Check the Pricing Dashboard for items with no active price (they will snapshot as ₫0).
3. Review the Summary page to confirm totals match physical records.
4. Reconcile all pending invoices.
5. Review the Audit Log for any unexpected actions.
